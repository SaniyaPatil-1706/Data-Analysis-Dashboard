#COVID-19 Pandemic Analysis Dashboard

##Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze COVID-19 pandemic data, including total cases, deaths, recoveries, active cases, vaccinations, ICU patients, and population. The dashboard helps understand the spread of COVID-19 across countries and cities, vaccination trends, variant distribution, and hospitalization patterns using Excel.

##Dataset Used
The dataset contains COVID-19 pandemic information related to countries, cities, cases, deaths, recoveries, vaccinations, hospitalizations, ICU patients, COVID-19 variants, population, months, and years. The dataset is used to analyze the overall impact of COVID-19 and identify important trends through an Excel dashboard.

##Process

###Data Collection
Used the COVID-19 pandemic dataset containing information about countries, cities, COVID-19 cases, deaths, recoveries, active cases, vaccinations, hospitalized patients, ICU patients, variants, population, months, and years.

###Data Preparation
Organized the raw COVID-19 data and prepared the required fields for analysis. Checked and structured country, city, month, year, variant, case, vaccination, hospitalization, and recovery-related data.

###Data Analysis
Analyzed Total Cases, Deaths, Recoveries, Active Cases, Vaccinations, ICU Patients, Hospitalized Patients, Population, COVID-19 Variants, Countries, Cities, Months, and Years.

###KPI Calculation
Calculated important metrics such as Total Cases, Total Deaths, Total Recoveries, Active Cases, Total Vaccinations, Recovery Rate, ICU Patients, and Population.

###Visual Analysis
Created charts to identify yearly vaccination trends, city-wise total cases, COVID-19 variant distribution, and the relationship between hospitalized and ICU patients.

###Dashboard Development
Created an interactive Excel dashboard containing KPI cards, charts, filters, and slicers. Country, Month, and Year filters allow users to explore the COVID-19 data based on different selections.
Business/Analytical Insights
Used the dashboard to understand the overall impact of COVID-19, vaccination progress, variant distribution, case concentration across cities, and hospitalization patterns.

###Business Insights
Overall Performance:
The dataset records approximately 3.07 billion total cases, 73.64 million deaths, and 2.62 billion recoveries. The dashboard also shows approximately 384.63 million active cases.

###Vaccination:
A total of approximately 18.02 billion vaccinations are recorded in the dataset, showing the large-scale vaccination activity during the pandemic period.

###Recovery Rate:
The overall recovery rate displayed on the dashboard is approximately 85.23%.

###Yearly Vaccination Trend:
The yearly vaccination trend shows changes in vaccination activity from 2020 to 2024, with the trend reaching its highest point around 2022 before declining in the following years.

###Variant Distribution:
The dashboard shows the distribution of different COVID-19 variants, including Alpha, Beta, Delta, Gamma, Omicron, and Other variants. This helps compare the presence of different variants within the dataset.

###City-wise Cases:
The city-wise chart compares total COVID-19 cases across selected cities, helping identify locations with comparatively higher case counts.

###Hospitalized vs ICU Patients:
The monthly comparison between hospitalized patients and ICU patients shows how hospitalization and critical-care requirements changed throughout the year.

###ICU Patients:
The dashboard records approximately 2.96 million ICU patients, providing an overview of the critical cases represented in the dataset.

##Final Conclusion
The COVID-19 Pandemic Analysis Dashboard provides a clear overview of the pandemic using important health and population indicators. It helps users understand the scale of COVID-19 cases, deaths, recoveries, vaccinations, variants, and hospitalization requirements.
The analysis shows the importance of monitoring case trends, vaccination progress, recovery rates, variant distribution, and ICU requirements. The dashboard can support data-driven understanding of pandemic patterns and help identify areas requiring further analysis or attention.
